01Where leads die

Five places a green checkmark hides a lost lead

  1. a

    Nobody owns the first reply

    The lead lands in a sheet and a channel. Everyone sees it. No one is assigned, so no one answers.

  2. b

    Filters that stop quietly

    An empty phone field or a changed form label sends the run down a branch that ends without an error.

  3. c

    Automations switched off

    A plan limit, an expired connection or a teammate's edit turns a workflow off. Nothing alerts you.

  4. d

    Timezone and date bugs

    Follow-ups scheduled for the wrong day, or after-hours leads that wait until Monday.

  5. e

    AI drafted it, nobody sent it

    The reply sits in drafts or a doc because the step that hands it to a human was never built.
