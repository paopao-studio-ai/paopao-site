Your privacy policy needs an automated-decisions section by 10 December 2026

Australian Privacy Principles 1.7–1.9 start on 10 December 2026. If software, AI or a rules engine makes or substantially helps make a decision that could significantly affect a person, and it uses their personal information, your APP privacy policy must say so.

APP 1.8 asks you to disclose three things:

  1. the kinds of personal information used by those computer programs;
  2. the kinds of decisions made solely by computer programs;
  3. the kinds of decisions where a program does something substantially and directly related to the decision (scoring, ranking, recommending, flagging).

Source: OAIC, 30 Sept 2026 and its fact sheet. The OAIC notes human review does not automatically take a decision out of scope, and suggests disclosing if in doubt.
